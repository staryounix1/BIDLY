Request → Offer → Job lifecycle (state machines)

DRAFT→ PUBLISHED→ MATCHING→ RECEIVING OFFERS→ PROVIDER SELECTED→ CONFIRMED→ IN PROGRESS→ COMPLETED→ PAID
Every transition is validated server-side. Off-path moves (e.g. CREATED → PAID) are rejected by the state machine and by database constraints.
